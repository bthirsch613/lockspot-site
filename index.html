Trace the exact shape of the place where you get work done. Walk in and the apps you picked lock themselves. Walk out and they're back.

How it works

  • Draw the real shape of your library, desk or classroom with one finger. Lockspot uses your shape, not a big circle.
  • Pick the apps that eat your afternoons with Apple's Screen Time picker.
  • Hold to lock in anywhere, no location needed.
  • A streak that forgives: one freeze a week, because life happens.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your zones, your app picks and your Screen Time choices stay on your iPhone. Blocking is free.
