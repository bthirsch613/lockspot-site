Claim my free month

For people new to Lockspot. It's a real Lockspot Pro subscription on the yearly plan, so Apple asks you to confirm with your Apple Account. After the free month, it renews at the yearly price shown on Apple's sheet, billed to your Apple Account, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the free month ends (Settings > your name > Subscriptions).

How to claim it

  1. Open this page on your iPhone and tap Claim my free month. If Lockspot isn't on your iPhone yet, the App Store asks you to download it first.
  2. Apple shows the offer and the yearly price it renews at. Confirm with your Apple Account to start your free month.
  3. Open Lockspot and sign in. If it still asks you to subscribe, tap Restore at the top left.

Already on Lockspot's membership screen? Tap Redeem code under the plans and type LOCKINWITHME.

Had Lockspot before, or Apple says the offer isn't for you? You can still get Lockspot on the App Store.
