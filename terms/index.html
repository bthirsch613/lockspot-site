2. What Lockspot does, and what it can't promise

Lockspot helps you keep your own focus. You draw zones on a map and pick apps. While your iPhone is inside a zone, or while you hold to lock in, Lockspot asks Apple's Screen Time to block those apps. Blocking works only while you have an active Lockspot Pro membership, and a free trial counts (see section 4).

You stay in control the whole time. You can stop blocking whenever you want, and none of these needs a membership:

  • end a lock-in with Hold to end;
  • delete a zone;
  • remove Lockspot's access in Settings > Screen Time;
  • turn off location for Lockspot (zones stop working; holding to lock in still works);
  • delete the app, which lifts every block.

Because you can always switch it off, and because it depends on Apple's Screen Time and location services, Lockspot doesn't guarantee that any app will be blocked at a given moment, or that it will stay blocked.

5. Location and timing limits

Zones depend on the location your iPhone reports, and Lockspot can't make that location more accurate.

  • GPS can drift, especially indoors, so a block can switch on or off near the edge of a zone. Drawing the zone a little larger than the building helps. Zones have a minimum size, and they can't tell the floors of a building apart.
  • With the app closed, a zone can take a few minutes to switch after you walk in or out. With the app open, it's usually quicker.
  • Zones need location access set to Always, with Precise Location on. Low Power Mode, turning off Background App Refresh, restarting your iPhone, weak signal and software updates can pause or delay zones.
  • Apple can change how Screen Time, location and background activity work in any software update, and that can change how Lockspot behaves.

6. What not to use Lockspot for

Lockspot isn't designed, tested or suitable for:

  • emergencies, or any situation where your safety or someone else's depends on reaching an app or keeping one blocked;
  • medical, mental-health or addiction treatment. Lockspot isn't a medical device and doesn't give medical advice;
  • parental control, or monitoring or restricting another person's phone. Lockspot works only for the person using the phone, and that person can turn it off;
  • any other safety-critical purpose, for example keeping you off your phone while driving.

Don't pick apps you might need for your safety, health, work or school. If you need a blocked app, end the lock-in or turn off Screen Time access. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.
