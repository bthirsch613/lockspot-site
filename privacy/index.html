Privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026 · Lockspot for iPhone, version 1

Lockspot is an iPhone app that blocks the apps you choose while you're inside places you draw on a map, or while you hold to lock in.

The short version

Lockspot doesn't collect any data about you. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything the app knows stays on your iPhone, and deleting the app deletes it.

What stays on your iPhone

Location. If you allow it, Lockspot uses your location to show you on the map and to check whether you're inside a zone you drew. With "Always" access, iOS wakes Lockspot near your zones so it can check in the background. Your location and the shapes you draw are processed and stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Screen Time choices. Lockspot uses Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls) to block the apps you pick. iOS gives Lockspot anonymous tokens for those apps, not their names, and Lockspot keeps them on your iPhone. Lockspot doesn't read or send your app usage, and Apple's rules don't allow it to.

Your activity in the app. Your zones, lock-in sessions, streaks, if-then plans and weekly recap are saved on your iPhone so the app works. They never leave it unless you choose to share your recap yourself, for example through the iOS share sheet.

Apple's services

The map is Apple Maps. To show map tiles, your iPhone requests them from Apple, and Apple handles those requests under its own privacy policy. If you choose to share crash reports with Apple in iOS settings, Apple may pass anonymous crash data to developers; Lockspot doesn't add any crash reporting of its own.

What we never do

  • We don't sell or share data, because we don't have any.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites.
  • We don't use advertising or analytics tools.
  • We don't need you to create an account.

Your controls

  • Turn off location: Settings > Lockspot > Location. Zones stop working; holding to lock in still works.
  • Turn off Screen Time access: in Settings > Screen Time, remove Lockspot's access. Blocking stops.
  • Delete everything: delete the Lockspot app. Every block is lifted and all data stored by the app is removed.

Children

Lockspot is a self-control tool for the person using the phone. It doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13, because it doesn't collect data at all.

Changes

If a future version adds features that send data off your iPhone (for example, friends features), we'll update this policy before that version ships and change the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open a question on GitHub. Please don't include personal details there, because questions are public.
